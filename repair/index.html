Rates and terms

Clear numbers before the wrench turns.

Every job starts with a diagnosis. The $10 diagnostic bench fee is credited toward the repair if you approve it. If the repair will cost more than $50, you can ask for a written estimate first, and we won't bill more than 10% over that estimate without your approval.

Standard repair labor carries a 30-day warranty against workmanship defects. Custom builds, race tuning, and customer-supplied parts carry no warranty. Performance and tuning work is for off-highway or competition use only.

Full payment is due before equipment is released. Equipment left 14 days after we notify you that it's done is subject to a $10/day storage fee, and items left past 60 days are treated as abandoned.
