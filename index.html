The Riot standard

We sell the build sheet, not the sticker.

Motorized bike buyers already know most storefronts are selling variations of the same open-catalog hardware. Riot Moto Co. starts from that reality and makes the hidden parts visible: mounting hardware, brake choices, bearing targets, fuel system notes, drive ratios, and service substitutions.

Every initial order is treated as a custom allocation. If a component cannot be sourced in a condition we are willing to support, the customer gets told before the build moves forward.
